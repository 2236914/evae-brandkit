Direction 01

Warm Apothecary.

Peach · Champagne · Umber · No blue
Deeply warm, ritualistic, longevity-forward. The "lab" is implied through molecular imagery and ingredient typography rather than colour. No blue at all — the cool counterpart to champagne is soft peach. References · Augustinus Bader · Aesop · La Mer

Direction 02

Elevated Champagne.

Cream · Champagne · Dove-blue · Balanced
The middle path. Warm cream and bone do 95% of the canvas, champagne lifts premium moments (badges, pull-quote accents, divider details), and a single refined dove-grey-blue plays the "labby trace" in glass mesh underlays and molecular SVGs. References · Noble Panacea · Sturm · Your Pinterest board

Direction 03

Refined Clinical.

Mist · Slate · Platinum · Lab-forward
Coolest of the three but still elevated — never icy. Platinum-grey replaces champagne as the metallic accent, dove-blue surface area doubles to ~5%, and the canvas takes a slightly cool tint. Reads as the laboratory, not the boutique. References · Sturm MC1 · Augustinus Bader Clinical · Lab UI
